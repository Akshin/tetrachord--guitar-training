## Play footer grouping (decided, not built yet)

Do not dump all play controls into one equal `flex-wrap` row. Group by decision, then put Play under the groups as a pedal:

1. **Ритм** — BPM knob as anchor, meter beside it, metronome as an icon toggle (not another Вкл/Выкл pair).
2. **Упражнение** — scheme-change interval. Even better as a small caption under the stage.
3. **Звук** — one shared **тональность** for melody and backing; melody and backing as icon toggles in that cluster. Key stays visible when backing is off.

Transport is centered below the clusters, always in the same place. On mobile: stage → Play → Звук → Ритм → scheme change. Do not mix metronome with melody just because both are toggles.
