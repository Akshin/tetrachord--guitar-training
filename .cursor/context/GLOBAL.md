## Play footer grouping

Three clusters, then Play as a pedal underneath:

1. **Ритм** — compact BPM knob, meter, metronome icon.
2. **Упражнение** — scheme-change dropdown + tabs (Нет / Гитара / Пианино) + **Отображение лада** toggle (names overlay under cards, on by default).
3. **Звук** — тональный центр, melody and backing off by default (key A).

On mobile: Play first, then Звук, Ритм, Упражнение.

Cluster and field titles use a Phosphor icon plus a label. Play/Stop is a single pedal. Controls sit in one glass console. Close (X) stows the console off-screen; a caret brings it back. Play stays visible.
